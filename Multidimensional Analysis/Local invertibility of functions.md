> <u>Definition</u>: Let $X, Y$ be two, Finitely-Dimensional normed Vector spaces, $U\subset X$ and $V\subset Y$ be oben subsets, $f:U\rightarrow V$ a Bijection. $f$ is Called a <u>Diffeomorphism</u>, if 
> $$f\in C^1(U,V) \text{ and } f^{-1} \in C^1(V,U)$$  The two subsets are called Diffeomorph to each other.

><u>Theorem</u>: Is $f: U \rightarrow V$ a Diffeomorphism, with the inverse function $g: V \rightarrow U$, So have the spaces $U\subset X$ and $V\subset Y$  the same Dimension. In the Respective point $x\in U$ and $y \in V$ ($y = f(x)$ and $x=g(y)$) holds: 
>$$ df(x) = (dg(y))^{-1},$$
>so that both differentials $df(x) : X \rightarrow Y$ and $dg(y):Y\rightarrow X$ are to each other inverse Functions.

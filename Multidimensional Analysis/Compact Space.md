Its the notion the seeks to generaliye the notion of a closed and bounded subset of euclidean space. 
The idea is that compact space has no punctures or missing endpoints
It was formally introduced by maurice Frechet in 1906 to generalize the [[Bolzano-Weierstrass]] theorem. 
For example any finite Space is compact.
But there are alos alternate definitions of compactness that is defined using covers consisting of open sets. This form of compactness holding for closed and bounded sets in Euclidean space is known as Heine-Borel theorem

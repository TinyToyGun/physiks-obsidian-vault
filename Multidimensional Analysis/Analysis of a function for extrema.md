1. Find critical Points of $f$: $df(x_0) = 0 \Leftrightarrow \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i} = 0, i= 1, ..., n$ 
2. calculate the Hessian matrix $H_f(x_0) = \left(\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_i \partial x_j}(x_0)\right)_{i,j=1}^n$. If it is a critical point not Semi-definit, then f has at that point no Lokal Extrema. Is $H_f(x_0)$ in a critical point Semi-Definit, then there is a Lokal Extrema at that point, with the Extrema being a Maximum or Minimum depending on the Negative or Positive-definit of the Matrix. Is The matrix Semi Definit then the Critical Point needs to be analyzed further.
3. Is the Definition Space $U$ of $f$ a [[Compact Space]], can you answer questions about the *Global* Extrema. For that we first analyze $f$ for Local Extrema on the inside of $U$, that means on all the Interior Point of $U = U \backslash \partial U$. Afterwards w eanalyze $f$ on the Border $\partial U$ of $U$. In the end the Maxima and Minima get compared and we can find out the Global Maxima.

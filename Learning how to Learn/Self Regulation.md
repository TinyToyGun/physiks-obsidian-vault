Using the right technique to inform Deep processing:
- Cue:
	- Build out the radar from deep learning
- Monitoring:
	- Is the Studying isolated or connected?
- Adjusting: 
	- Adjust your technique, so that the processing becomes deeper again
A very common pitfall, is that by adjusting your technique, the learning becomes harder, and that tricks the brain into thinking that is worse, Whilst it can of course be true that the technique might not be right, often it is also just a reaction to habit change.
